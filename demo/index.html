AI detector plugin demo

Press Check for AI writing in the bar under any editor below. TinyMCE and HugeRTE also have the check on the toolbar. The page replays a recorded answer from the AssetFeed detector for the sample text, so it works without an API key. Edited text needs a real key.

Sample text: the opening of Three Men in a Boat by Jerome K. Jerome (1889, public domain), followed by three paragraphs written by an AI model.

To check your own text, connect one of the plugins to an AssetFeed account. Free accounts include 500 credits per month, and one check uses 5 credits. Get an API key or see plans. Without a key, paste the text into our detector page, free while in beta.
